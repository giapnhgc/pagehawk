Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

PageHawk is a browser extension that checks web pages you choose and notifies you when they change. It is built to work without collecting your data.

What stays on your computer

The list of pages you watch, the text snapshots of those pages, change history and settings are stored only in your browser’s local extension storage. We do not operate servers that receive this information, and we cannot see it.

Website access

PageHawk asks for permission to access a website only when you choose to watch a page on it. It then downloads that page directly from the website, from your browser, at the interval you set. If you enable “Use my login cookies”, the request includes your existing browser cookies for that site, the same as when you visit it yourself. You can revoke access at any time in Chrome’s extension settings.

Pro license

If you buy PageHawk Pro, payments are processed by Gumroad (our merchant of record), which receives the information you provide at checkout under its own privacy policy. When you enter a license key, the extension sends the key to Gumroad’s license API to activate it and, periodically, to check that it is still valid. The email linked to your license is shown to you in the extension and stored locally.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No remote code: all code ships inside the extension package.

Deleting your data

Delete monitors in the dashboard, or uninstall the extension to remove all locally stored data.
